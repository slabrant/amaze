Which terms steer the choice

Each step is picked from many candidates by the sum of its terms. Lift is how far the chosen step's term sits above the average candidate's, averaged per seed, with a 95% interval across seeds. A term whose interval crosses zero is not steering the choice. Decides is how often removing the term would change which step wins.
